Medical Red Flags & Life-Safety Protocols

When these signs appear, self-medication must be avoided and immediate physician/hospital care is mandatory.

1. Brain Stroke — F.A.S.T. Protocol

Every minute of delay destroys 1.9 million brain neurons. Check:

  • F (Face): Does one side of the face droop when smiling?
  • A (Arms): Does one arm drift downward when raised?
  • S (Speech): Is speech slurred, garbled, or unable to speak?
  • T (Time): Call 112 / 108 / 911 immediately! Do NOT give aspirin until CT rules out bleeding.

2. Heart Attack vs. Heartburn

Never dismiss crushing chest pressure as simple gastric acidity:

  • Heart Attack: Squeezing/crushing center chest tightness radiating to left arm, neck, or jaw + cold sweats & breathlessness.
  • Heartburn: Rising burning feeling after spicy/oily meals, sour taste, relieved by antacids.
  • Action: Call Ambulance immediately; chew 300mg Aspirin if advised and not allergic.

3. Acute Meningitis & Thunderclap Headache

  • Sudden explosive "worst headache of life" reaches peak within 60 seconds.
  • High fever with stiff neck (unable to touch chin to chest) & photophobia.
  • Tumbler Test: Red/purple skin spots that do NOT fade when a clear glass is pressed against them.

4. Anaphylaxis & Airway Obstruction

  • Swelling of tongue, lips, or throat with hoarse voice or stridor wheezing.
  • Blue tint around lips/fingertips (cyanosis) or gasping for air.
  • Action: Inject Epinephrine (EpiPen) into outer mid-thigh and call 112/108.
